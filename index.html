About Sea Breeze

Welcome to Sea Breeze Roof Top Restaurant — the perfect rooftop dining destination at Devka Beach Resort, where delicious food, refreshing sea breeze, and stunning sunset views come together for an unforgettable experience.

Whether you are planning a relaxed family meal, a romantic candlelight dinner, or a special celebration by the sea, our rooftop setting brings together warm hospitality, fresh flavors, and a calm coastal atmosphere.

From freshly prepared starters to hearty main courses, refreshing mocktails, and sweet desserts, every visit is designed to feel comfortable, memorable, and full of flavor. The open-air rooftop ambience makes it a beautiful place to enjoy lunch, dinner, sunsets, weekend moments, and private occasions with your favorite people.
